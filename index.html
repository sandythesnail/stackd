FAQ

Questions? We've got answers

How long does each module take?

About 10 minutes. Each module includes in-depth lessons, interactive scenarios, quizzes, and assessments, and you can work through them at your own pace and pick up where you left off.

Do I need any financial background to use Stacked?

Not at all. Stacked was built for students who are starting from zero. Every concept is explained from the ground up with real campus examples. No jargon, no prerequisites.

Is my progress saved?

Yes. Your XP, badges, streaks, and module progress are all saved to your account. Pick up right where you left off anytime.

Is Stacked financial advice?

No. Stacked is education. We are not financial advisers, brokers, tax preparers or lenders, and nothing in the app is personalised advice or a recommendation about your actual money. Lessons use simplified examples, and the tools work only on numbers you type in yourself — they connect to no bank. For a real decision, talk to a qualified professional.
